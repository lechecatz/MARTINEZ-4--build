About Us

'Teacups and Cats' is a cat cafe located in Meow City, New Meowxico. We strive to provide a safe and “pawsitive” environment for our cats while also providing a warm, welcoming space for our human guests as well! Our cafe offers a variety of teas and pastries for you to enjoy while interacting and spending quality time with our feline friends. We also have a dedicated adoption program and hope to find them all loving homes.

Did you know?

Approximately 3 million cats enter shelters and rescues every single year. And about 277,000 of them face euthanasia that same year. That's a big number! And it doesn't even include the number of stray cats still roaming the streets outside.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a safe and enriching environment for our rescue cats and to provide the opportunity for potential adopters to interact with them in a warm and cozy environment. We hope for our cats to all someday find their forever homes. If you're interested in adopting one of our cats, please visit our 'adopt' page for more information.
